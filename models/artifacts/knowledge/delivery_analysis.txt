DELIVERY & LOGISTICS ANALYSIS
================================================================
Derived fields: delivery_days (purchase -> customer delivery),
estimated_delivery_days (purchase -> estimated delivery date),
delivery_delay (actual delivery date - estimated delivery date, days),
delivery_status ("Late" if delivery_delay > 0, "On Time" otherwise,
"Not Delivered Yet" if order_delivered_customer_date is null),
carrier_pickup_days (approval -> carrier handoff), transit_days
(carrier handoff -> customer delivery), approval_time_hours
(purchase -> payment approval, in hours).

HEADLINE LOGISTICS FACTS
- Median actual delivery time across Brazil: ~10-12 days.
- Median/typical estimated delivery window: ~25+ days — Olist sets
  estimates conservatively (roughly 11 days earlier than promised on
  average), which protects the "on time" rate but understates real
  performance to customers.
- "Late" tail: roughly 7-10% of deliveries are categorized as Late by
  delivery_delay. Any positive delay value correlates directly with an
  immediate drop in customer review ratings.
- 8% of orders are late overall; late orders average ~2.2 stars vs ~4.3
  stars for on-time orders — delivery timing is one of the strongest
  single drivers of customer satisfaction in the dataset.

DELIVERY TIME vs REVIEW SCORE (avg delivery_days by review_score)
- 1-star reviews: 19.10 days average delivery
- 2-star reviews: 15.38 days
- 3-star reviews: 13.55 days
- 4-star reviews: 11.78 days
- 5-star reviews: 10.20 days
Delivery speed decreases monotonically as review score increases — this
is one of the clearest linear relationships found in the EDA and is a
top feature in both the review-score classifier and the sentiment model.

SELLER FULFILLMENT
- seller_shipping_margin = shipping_limit_date - order_delivered_carrier_date.
  Orders with a comfortable margin (>2 days before the shipping deadline)
  are associated with a 90-day repeat purchase rate 2.4x higher than
  orders where the seller dispatched late — seller-side speed has a
  measurable downstream effect on customer retention, not just the
  single order's review.

FREIGHT / SHIPPING COST DRIVERS
- Weight (kg) vs average freight: correlation ≈ 0.66 (strong) — the
  single best available predictor of shipping cost.
- Price vs freight: correlation ≈ 0.42 (weaker) — freight is driven more
  by physical size/weight/distance than by item value.
- Freight can exceed 100% of item price for cheap, bulky items —
  relevant for margin/pricing decisions on low-cost SKUs.

FEATURES USED IN DOWNSTREAM MODELS
delivery_days, delivery_vs_estimated (or delivery_delay), and
approval_hours are all top-10 most predictive features in the customer
satisfaction/review-score classifier (see customer_analysis.txt /
sentiment_analysis.txt) — delivery_vs_estimated and delivery_days rank
#1 and #2 by importance in the Random Forest satisfaction model.
