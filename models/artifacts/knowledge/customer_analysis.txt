CUSTOMER ANALYSIS (RFM & SEGMENTATION)
================================================================
Built with a classic RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) model on
customer_unique_id, snapshot date = max(order_purchase_timestamp) + 1 day.

BASE STATS (96,096 unique customers)
- Mean recency: 288.7 days since last purchase (std 153.4)
- Mean frequency: 1.03 orders/customer (median 1, max 17)
- Mean monetary value: R$214.16 (median R$113.34, max R$109,312.64)
- Mean average order value: R$206.07

REPEAT PURCHASE RATE
- Only 3.1% of customers made more than one purchase (is_repeat_customer).
- This is the single biggest flagged business opportunity in the whole
  EDA: retention/re-engagement, not acquisition, is where the platform
  recommends focusing.

RFM SCORING & SEGMENTS
R, F, M each scored 1-5 via quintiles; RFM_score = R+F+M; segment rules:
- RFM_score >= 12: "Champions"
- RFM_score >= 9: "Loyal"
- RFM_score >= 6: "At Risk"
- else: "Lost"

Segment summary (customers, avg monetary, total revenue):
- Loyal: 39,125 customers, avg R$234.96, total revenue R$9,192,893.60
- Champions: 15,988 customers, avg R$421.31, total revenue R$6,735,977.02
- At Risk: 32,917 customers, avg R$127.25, total revenue R$4,188,725.64
- Lost: 8,066 customers, avg R$57.29, total revenue R$462,067.75

Interpretation: Loyal + Champions (55,113 customers, ~57% of the base)
generate about R$15.9M, roughly 77% of total revenue, even though
Champions alone are only ~17% of customers — a small high-value segment
drives a disproportionate share of revenue and is the natural target for
loyalty/VIP programs. At Risk customers are a large group (34% of
customers) with meaningfully lower average spend — a natural
re-engagement/win-back campaign target.

GEOGRAPHY
- Customer counts are concentrated in SP, RJ, MG (see sales_kpis.txt for
  revenue-by-state figures — the same three states dominate both revenue
  and customer count).
- Top cities by customer count: São Paulo (18,875), Rio de Janeiro (8,311),
  Belo Horizonte (3,299), Brasília (2,500), Curitiba (1,827), Campinas
  (1,757), Porto Alegre (1,675), Salvador (1,544), Guarulhos (1,415),
  São Bernardo do Campo (1,131).

SELLER-SIDE INSIGHT LINKED TO CUSTOMER BEHAVIOR
Orders dispatched with a high margin of safety (seller_shipping_margin
> 2 days, i.e. sellers ship well ahead of their shipping_limit_date)
achieve a 90-day repeat purchase rate 2.4x higher than orders where the
seller's dispatch was delayed — seller fulfillment speed is strongly
linked to whether a customer comes back.

DOWNSTREAM USE
RFM/segment fields (rfm.pkl) feed the customer-segmentation views in the
dashboard and are a natural grounding source for the GenAI insights layer
when answering questions like "who are our most valuable customers" or
"which segment should we target for a win-back campaign."
